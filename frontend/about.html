A Little Bit of History

From a small neighborhood café to a modern favorite — without losing what made it special.

Brew & Bean began in 1998 as a small neighborhood café with three wooden tables and a single espresso machine. Word spread slowly, cup by cup, until the little shop with the big heart became a daily ritual for the whole street.

Today, Brew & Bean has grown into a modern café — but the beans are still roasted with the same care, and the welcome is still just as warm.

1998
Opened as a three-table neighborhood café.
2006
Introduced our signature Filter Coffee blend.
2015
Renovated into our current warm, wood-and-brass interior.
2024
Launched online ordering, without losing the café feeling.
